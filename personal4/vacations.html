Vacations

Santorini, Greece

I would like to visit Santorini because the white hillside buildings, blue water, and sunsets look peaceful and beautiful. I would enjoy walking through the small streets, trying Greek food, and seeing the Aegean Sea in person.

Tokyo, Japan

Tokyo interests me because it combines modern technology with traditional culture. I would like to explore the city lights, ride the trains, visit museums and temples, and try authentic Japanese food.

The Maldives

I would like to visit the Maldives for its clear water, quiet beaches, and relaxing atmosphere. It would be a great place to swim, snorkel, and take a break from a busy schedule.

Paris, France

Paris is on my list because of its architecture, museums, food, and history. I would especially like to see the Eiffel Tower, walk near the Seine, and spend time exploring the Louvre.
